# AI Tools Directory (2026)

A curated directory of AI tools for developers, designers, researchers, creators, and teams.

The repository separates tools into two lists so contributors can distinguish tools with usable free access from commercial products and paid plans.

## Browse the directory

- [Free AI Tools](FREE-TOOLS.md) — open-source tools and products with a free plan or free usage tier.
- [Paid AI Tools](PAID-TOOLS.md) — commercial products where paid plans, credits, or usage-based billing are central to normal use.
- [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) — how to suggest a tool or update an entry.

## Important pricing note

“Free” does not mean unlimited, unrestricted, or free for commercial use. Some free offerings have rate limits, monthly quotas, watermarks, hardware requirements, or separate charges for model/API usage and hosting. Likewise, some products in the paid list may offer trials or limited free plans.

Pricing and plan names change frequently. Please check each vendor's official pricing and terms before relying on an entry. This repository does not guarantee that a listed tool currently has a particular quota or price.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome! Please submit a pull request with:
- the tool name and official website;
- its category and a concise, factual description;
- whether it is open source, free tier, freemium, subscription-based, or usage-based;
- a link to official pricing or licensing information, where available.

Please avoid duplicate entries, referral links, and unsupported claims such as “100% free” or “unlimited.”

Happy building! 🚀
