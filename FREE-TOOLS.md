## Before using a free tool

- Check whether the free plan permits commercial use.
- Check API quotas, rate limits, and model-specific restrictions.
- For self-hosted software, account for compute, storage, and hosting costs.
- Do not upload confidential company or customer data without reviewing privacy and retention terms.
